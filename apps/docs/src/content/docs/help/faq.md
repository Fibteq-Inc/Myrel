### Does a task run in the cloud or on my computer?

It depends on the project's **Run tasks** setting. Under each reply, **Cloud** or **Your computer** shows where it ran. See [Folders and where tasks run](/computer/folders/).

### What does "Estimated cost" mean?

The model usage of that reply at list prices. Work on your own computer uses your CLI's login and shows no cost. See [Plans, usage and costs](/account/usage/).

### Can I use my own model key?

Yes. **Settings** → **Model keys** → **Add a key**, then pick it in **Project settings** → **Model and key**.

### What language does Myrel answer in?

The one you write in. The **Language** setting only changes menus and labels.

### Can I delete a thread?

No. Threads are resolved or closed, never deleted. See [Resolve, close and reopen](/work/finishing/).

### Who can see my project?

Only its members. Invite people under **Project settings** → **Members**.

### I run my own Myrel server. Where are the setup docs?

In the repository: the [self-hosting guide](https://github.com/Fibteq-Inc/myrel/blob/main/docs/self-hosting.md).
