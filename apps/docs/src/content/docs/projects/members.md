Everyone in a project sees the same chat and threads, can ask for work and can answer Myrel's questions.

## Invite someone

1. Open **Project settings** → **Members**.
2. Choose **Invite people**.
3. Enter their email, pick a role and choose **Invite**.

They accept under **Settings** → **Invitations** with **Join**. If your server has closed sign-ups, invited people can still create an account.

Pending invitations show under Members; **Revoke** cancels one.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including deleting the project |
| Admin | Manage members, invitations and project settings |
| Member | Ask for work, answer questions, use the project's files |

## Leave or remove

- To leave a project: **Members** → **Leave project**.
- Owners and admins remove someone with **Remove** next to their name.
