---
title: Resolve, close and reopen
description: Finish a task, or bring it back.
---

## Resolve

When a task is done, choose **Mark resolved** at the top of the thread. It moves to the **Resolved** group in the sidebar. Myrel also marks a thread resolved when you confirm the result.

## Reopen

Open a resolved thread and choose **Reopen thread**, or just write in it. It continues with everything it knew, and starts a new progress list for the new work.

## Closed threads

Sometimes the coordinator closes a thread it opened by mistake, for example a duplicate of another ask. It shows **Closed as a duplicate** or **Closed, another thread has it**. Nothing is deleted; write in it to reopen it.

## Can I delete a thread?

No. Threads are resolved or closed, never deleted, so the project keeps its history.
