Myrel works on its own and comes back when something is yours to decide. The thread then shows **Waiting for you** and you get a **Needs your decision** notification.

## Decision cards

A **Your call** card asks one question with two to four options. Each option says what will happen if you pick it, and one is marked **recommended**.

- Choose an option to answer. The card then shows what was chosen and Myrel carries on.
- Prefer something else? Write it in **Reply in this thread** instead.

## Reviews

When Myrel shows you something to look at (a draft, a proposal, an artifact), the thread waits for your feedback.

- Happy with it: say so, or choose **Mark resolved** if nothing else is needed.
- Want changes: reply with what to change. Be specific: "shorten the intro and drop the pricing table" works better than "make it better".

## Permission cards

When a task on your computer needs something outside its folders, or a command it is not allowed to run on its own, it asks with a card. See [Approve access](/computer/approvals/).
