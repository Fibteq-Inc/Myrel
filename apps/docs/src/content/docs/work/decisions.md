Myrel works on its own and comes back when something is yours to decide. The thread then shows **Waiting for you** and you get a **Needs your decision** notification.

## Decision cards

A **Your call** card asks one question with two to four options. Each option says what will happen if you pick it, and one is marked **recommended**.

- Choose an option to answer. The card then shows what was chosen and Myrel carries on.
- Prefer something else? Write it in **Reply in this thread** instead.

When there are several cards in a thread, each one takes only its own answer; the others stay open until you answer them.

## Several questions at once

When Myrel needs a few decisions before it can go on, it asks them together in one card and you answer them one by one. The card shows **Question 1 of 3** and opens the next question as soon as you choose; the questions you answered stay at the top with your choice, and the ones still to come are listed below.

Myrel picks up the work once you answered the last one, with every answer tied to its question. Writing your own reply in the thread instead closes the questions still open, and Myrel reads your message.

## Questions the coordinator answers for you

Before a decision card reaches you, the project's coordinator looks at it first. If your project instructions, Myrel's memory or a decision you already made settle the question, it answers for you and the task goes on without waiting. The thread then shows a line like:

> The coordinator answered the agent's question: **Lazy-load it** (source: project instructions)

Open **Message sent** under it to see the original question. If the answer isn't what you want, reply in the thread and Myrel changes course.

Everything else still comes to you as a **Your call** card, sometimes with a line of context from the coordinator. When Myrel asked several questions together, the coordinator looks at each one on its own: it answers the ones it can and brings you the rest in one card. Questions that only you can answer (approving something that can't be undone, matters of taste, work you asked to check yourself) always come to you.

To have more questions answered for you, write your preferences in the [project instructions](/projects/knowledge/).

## Reviews

When Myrel shows you something to look at (a draft, a proposal, an artifact), the thread waits for your feedback.

- Happy with it: say so, or choose **Mark resolved** if nothing else is needed.
- Want changes: reply with what to change. Be specific: "shorten the intro and drop the pricing table" works better than "make it better".

## Permission cards

When a task on your computer needs something outside its folders, or a command it is not allowed to run on its own, it asks with a card. See [Approve access](/computer/approvals/).
