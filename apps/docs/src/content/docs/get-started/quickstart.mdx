In five minutes you will create a project, ask for a task and see how Myrel works on it. You need an account on [myrel.fibteq.io](https://myrel.fibteq.io) or on your own Myrel server.

## What you just did

1. **Created a project.** Everything for one piece of work lives here: the chat, the threads, the files and what Myrel remembers.
2. **Asked in the chat.** You don't need to plan the work yourself. Myrel turns each ask into its own thread.
3. **Followed a thread.** Each thread has its own progress list and its own replies, so several tasks can run at once without mixing up.
4. **Answered a decision.** Myrel goes on by itself and comes back to you when something is yours to decide.
