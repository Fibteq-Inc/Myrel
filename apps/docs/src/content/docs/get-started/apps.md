---
title: Get the apps
description: Use Myrel in the browser, on your desktop or on Android.
---

Your projects are the same everywhere: start a task on your laptop and answer its question on your phone.

## Web

Open [myrel.fibteq.io](https://myrel.fibteq.io) and sign in with your email and password. To create an account choose **Create an account**.

## Desktop (macOS, Windows, Linux)

1. Download the installer for your system from the [latest release](https://github.com/Fibteq-Inc/myrel/releases/latest): `.dmg` for macOS, `.exe` or `.msi` for Windows, `.AppImage`, `.deb` or `.rpm` for Linux.
2. Open the app and sign in.

The desktop app updates itself and can connect your computer in one click, without a terminal. See [Connect a computer](/computer/connect/).

:::note[Windows]
The Windows installer is not signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Choose **More info** and then **Run anyway**.
:::

## Android

Install Myrel from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.fibteq.myrel). The app sends a push notification when a task finishes or needs you, once you allow notifications.

To change a project's settings on your phone, open the project and tap the gear at the top right. The app covers the project's name, where tasks run, the models on your computer, GitHub repositories, members and invitations. Folders, connectors, skills and the model key are set on the web; **Open in browser** at the bottom of the screen takes you there.

## Using your own server

The apps connect to myrel.fibteq.io by default. If your team runs its own Myrel:

1. On the sign-in screen, choose **Change** next to the server name.
2. Enter your server's address under **Myrel server** and choose **Connect**.
