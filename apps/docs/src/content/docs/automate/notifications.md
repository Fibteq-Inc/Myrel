The bell in the left rail collects what happened while you were away.

| Notification | When |
|---|---|
| **Finished** | A thread is done |
| **New reply** | Myrel replied in a thread |
| **Needs your decision** | A thread asks you something |
| **Blocked** | A thread can't go on without you |
| **Failed** | A task stopped with an error |

**Mark all read** clears them.

## Email and browser notifications

At the bottom of the bell's panel:

- **Email me when a thread finishes or needs me**: email for Finished, Needs your decision, Blocked and Failed (not for every reply).
- **Browser notifications on this device**: notifications from this browser, even when the tab is in the background.

Some self-hosted servers don't send email; then the email switch isn't shown.

## Phone and desktop

The Android app sends push notifications once you allow them. The desktop app uses your system's notifications.
